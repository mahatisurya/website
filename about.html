Our Story

Rooted in Purpose. Built for Scale.

Established in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, and serving ambitious clients across India and internationally, QQadz brings together a passionate collective of young Indian strategists, marketers, designers, developers, and filmmakers.

Rather than operating in fragmented silos where the marketing team doesn't talk to the developers, we work as one unified engine. When we build a website, it is engineered for SEO and conversions from day one. When we design a brand identity, it is crafted with digital application and motion in mind.

Over the years, we have had the privilege of partnering with premier educational institutions like Aditya Educational Institutions and Technical Hub, global healthcare providers like Coral Orthodontics USA, industrial leaders like Suresh Engineering, and esteemed professional bodies like ICAI Kakinada.
