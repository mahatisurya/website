# QQadz — Professional Agency Website

A premium, modern, and high-converting website for **QQadz**, a digital marketing, branding, technology, and creative agency based in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, India.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Brand System

- **Palette Ratio:** 60% White (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`), 30% QQ Blue (`#00ABF0`, `#007DB5`), 10% Black/Charcoal (`#0F172A`, `#32494F`).
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter (Google Fonts).
- **Team Visuals:** Authentic young Indian professionals (aged 22–35) in a modern Indian agency office environment with subtle `#00ABF0` accents.

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Mobile-First & Fully Responsive:** Hamburger drawer navigation, touch-friendly tap targets, and adaptive layouts across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
2. **Interactive Portfolio Filters:** Filter projects dynamically by *All*, *Digital Marketing*, *Branding*, *Websites*, *Creative Campaigns*, and *Corporate Films*.
3. **Floating WhatsApp Widget:** Persistent on every page with direct pre-filled messaging to `+91 6269626926`.
4. **Authentic Client Case Studies:**
   - Aditya Educational Institutions
   - Technical Hub
   - Coral Orthodontics USA
   - Suresh Engineering & Infrastructure Co.
   - ICAI Kakinada
5. **SEO & Accessibility Ready:** Semantic HTML5, ARIA labels, meta tags, and high-contrast accessibility focus states.
